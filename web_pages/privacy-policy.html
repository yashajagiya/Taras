Privacy Policy for Taras

Absolute on-device privacy. We collect zero digital personal data on any server. Your preferences and digital card binder exist exclusively on your physical phone in secure local sandboxes.

Effective: October 8, 2026
Jurisdiction: Republic of India
Framework: DPDPA 2023 & IT Act 2000

1. Compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA 2023)

In compliance with the statutory provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 enacted by the Parliament of the Republic of India:

  • Data Fiduciary: Yash Ajagiya acts as the statutory Data Fiduciary for Taras.
  • Purpose Limitation (Section 6): Preferences entered (nickname, favorite driver/team) are processed solely on-device to personalize local displays and home-screen widgets.
  • Data Minimization: The app operates with zero user registration, zero cloud accounts, zero phone numbers, and zero email capture.
  • Zero Server Ingestion: No personal identifier is ever transmitted to, stored on, or processed by external cloud infrastructure.

2. Information We Never Collect

Taras is engineered to be 100% telemetry-free:

  • No Personal Identifiers: No real names, emails, phone numbers, or contacts.
  • No Location Tracking: No GPS, cell tower, or Wi-Fi geo-tracking.
  • No Device Identifiers: No GAID, IMEI, MAC address, or IP logging.
  • No Ad Networks or Analytics: Zero Firebase Analytics, AdMob, Mixpanel, or Segment.

3. Strictly Local On-Device Storage

All preferences and game progress are stored strictly inside Android's sandboxed local container:

Data Tech Transmitted?
Nickname & Team/Driver Jetpack DataStore Never
Theme & Language Jetpack DataStore Never
F1 GridTCG Cards & Binder SQLite (Room DB) Never
Offline Schedule Cache SQLite (Room DB) Never

4. Statutory Rights of the Data Principal (Sections 11–14, DPDPA 2023)

Right to Access (Sec. 11)

All data stored is directly visible to you inside the app settings, drawer, and card binder.

Right to Correction (Sec. 12)

Easily modify your nickname, favorite driver, or theme at any moment in the Settings Drawer.

Right to Erasure (Sec. 12)

Clear all data instantly via Android Settings > Apps > Taras > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling.

Grievance Redressal (Sec. 13)

Direct access to our designated Grievance Officer with statutory resolution within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy (Section 9, DPDPA 2023)

Taras does not process personal data of children, does not monitor behavioral activity, and does not serve targeted advertisements. It is fully suitable and family-friendly for motorsport enthusiasts of all ages.

6. Network Communications & Feeds

All network traffic is conducted over TLS 1.3 / HTTPS. Public schedule data is fetched from static GitHub repositories. News articles are parsed on-device as an intermediary reader under Section 2(1)(w) of the IT Act 2000.
