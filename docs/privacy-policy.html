🏎️ Taras F1 Companion No Data Collected

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026 • Version 1.0

Core Commitment

We collect no personal data. Your name and favorite driver preferences are stored locally on your device via Android Jetpack DataStore. No personal data is sent to any server. News is fetched directly from public RSS feeds.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Taras is an open-source, non-commercial Formula 1 companion application built by Yash Ajagiya. We believe in complete transparency and maximum privacy.

  • We do not collect your real identity, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • We do not track your precise or coarse geographic location.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers (GAID), unique device hardware IDs, or IP addresses.
  • We do not record your browsing history, race search queries, or reading activity.
  • We do not include any commercial advertising SDKs, marketing trackers, or third-party telemetry libraries.

2. On-Device Storage (Local Only)

To provide a personalized experience with home-screen widgets and custom dashboards, the app stores a few configuration preferences strictly locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore and Room SQLite database:

Data Purpose Storage Location Transmitted?
Nickname / Name Greeting on the dashboard & widgets Local DataStore Never
Favorite Driver & Team Highlighting favorite driver widgets Local DataStore Never
Theme / Language Preference App display appearance (Light/Dark/Language) Local DataStore Never
Race Standings & Schedule Cache Offline viewing & fast app loading Local Room DB Never

All local preferences can be wiped at any time by clearing the application storage via Android Settings → Apps → Taras → Storage → Clear Data or simply by uninstalling the application.

3. Network Communications & Public Feeds

Taras interacts with the internet strictly to retrieve public Formula 1 sports information:

  • Public Sports Data (TarasF1Data): Race schedules, driver standings, constructor standings, and circuit information are fetched from the public static GitHub repository (tarasF1Data). No client credentials, user identifiers, or personal payload are transmitted.
  • News Aggregator: News headlines and thumbnails are parsed on-device directly from public RSS feeds published by prominent motorsport journalism outlets (such as Motorsport.com, Autosport, RaceFans, and PlanetF1).
  • All Connections over HTTPS: All network interactions are encrypted in transit via standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

4. Device Permissions

Taras requests minimal Android permissions only when necessary for user-facing features:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): On Android 13 and above, you may optionally enable notification permissions to receive race weekend countdown reminders and schedule notifications scheduled locally by Android WorkManager. No remote push notification tokens or device IDs are collected or stored.

5. Google Play Data Safety Summary

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and the Google Play Data Safety Declaration:

  • Data Collection: No user data is collected by the developer.
  • Data Sharing: No user data is shared with third parties or advertisers.
  • Data Transfer Security: All public API network requests are performed over secure HTTPS (encrypted in transit).
  • Data Deletion: Users have 100% control over their data; uninstalling the app or clearing app cache permanently removes all stored data.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Taras does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the application does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it is safe for Formula 1 fans of all ages.
