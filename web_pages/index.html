The Meaning of "Taras" (तरस्)

In Sanskrit, "Taras" (तरस्) translates to "Speed," "Velocity," "Energy," "Impetus," or "Strength." It mirrors the raw physics and split-second precision of Formula 1 motorsport.

Most modern sports apps are weighed down with unskippable video ads, third-party analytics trackers, and heavy cloud sign-ins. Taras was architected to be the exact opposite: lightweight, instant, and respectful of your phone's battery.

Sanskrit: तरस् Pure Velocity Zero Bloat

100% On-Device Privacy
