The Meaning & Philosophy of "Taras"

In Sanskrit, "Taras" (तरस्) translates to "Speed," "Velocity," "Dynamic Energy," "Impetus," or "Strength."

It reflects the core essence of Formula 1: cutting-edge aerodynamics, milliseconds of decision-making, and high-octane engineering. We believe sports companion apps should be as lean and responsive as an F1 machine.

Sanskrit तरस् Velocity Purity of Speed

Developer & Motivation

Created by Yash Ajagiya, an independent Android engineer and Formula 1 enthusiast from Gujarat, India.

Frustrated by sports apps cluttered with intrusive 30-second video ads, forced cloud account logins, and battery-draining trackers, Taras was created to bring back respect for user privacy, device battery, and clean aesthetic design.
